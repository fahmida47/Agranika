# 🌱 Agranika – A Web Platform to Support Education for Underprivileged Children

## 📌 Project Overview

**Agranika** is a MERN Stack based web application designed to support the education of underprivileged children by connecting donors, sponsors, volunteers, and educational organizations through a centralized digital platform.

The platform aims to improve transparency, accessibility, and efficiency in managing educational sponsorships, donations, and child development records.

---

## 🚀 Problem Statement

Education is a fundamental human right, yet many underprivileged children still lack access to proper educational support and resources.

Children from rural and low-income communities often face challenges such as:

* Lack of financial support
* Limited access to learning materials
* Insufficient educational guidance
* Digital divide and poor connectivity

### ❗ Who is Affected?

* Underprivileged children
* Low-income families
* NGOs and educational organizations

### ⚠ Current Challenges

* No centralized platform for sponsors and organizations
* Manual data management systems
* Lack of donation transparency
* Limited visibility of children needing support

### ❌ Why Existing Solutions Are Not Enough

Most current systems are either offline or fragmented. They fail to provide an integrated digital platform where organizations can manage child profiles, sponsorships, donations, and educational progress in one place.

---

# 💡 Proposed Solution

Agranika provides a modern web-based solution where organizations can digitally manage educational support activities while sponsors and donors can easily contribute to children's education.

The system focuses on:

* Transparency
* Ease of use
* Efficient data management
* Secure sponsorship process

# ✨ Key Features

## 🔐 User Authentication & Authorization

* Secure Login & Registration
* JWT-based Authentication
* Role-based Access Control (Admin, Sponsor, Organization)

## 👦 Child Profile Management

* Create Child Profiles
* Update Educational Information
* Manage Sponsorship Status

## 💰 Sponsorship & Donation System

* Sponsor a Child
* Donation Tracking
* Donation History

## 🔍 Search & Filter

* Search Children by Age
* Filter Sponsored & Unsponsored Children

## 📊 Admin Dashboard

* Manage Users
* Manage Child Profiles
* Monitor Donations & Reports

## 🔔 Notification System

* Sponsorship Update Notifications
* Donation Alerts
* Profile Update Notifications
